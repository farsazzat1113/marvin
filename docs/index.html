QUESTIONS

FAQ.

Does it teleport my character?

No. MARVIN SOLO never writes your character's CFrame/Position and never uses velocity exploits. Movement is real Humanoid walking with trap avoidance — slower than speed-hack hubs, but dramatically safer.

Is there a key system?

No. MARVIN SOLO is free and keyless — paste the loader, run it, and the finder is already on your screen. Nothing to unlock, nothing to revalidate after server hops.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The whole interface is touch-sized, draggable, and adapts to portrait and landscape. Desktop gets a resizable panel.

Where do the egg reports come from?

From other players running MARVIN SOLO. Every report is validated for expiry, server ID and value before it's shown — taken or full servers are marked, not silently dropped.

What executors are supported?

Any executor with loadstring, HTTP requests, and (optionally) teleport-queue for Auto Execute after a hop. Without a queue API, Auto Join still works — you'll just re-run the loader manually in the new server.
